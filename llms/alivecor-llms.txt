# AliveCor

> AliveCor builds FDA-cleared, AI-powered personal ECG hardware (KardiaMobile 6L, KardiaMobile Card, Kardia 12L) and digital cardiac-care software (KardiaPro, KardiaStation, KardiaComplete) for consumers, clinicians, health systems, biopharma, and payers/employers. For developers and partners it offers a data-integration layer: mobile SDKs (Android/iOS) that embed its FDA-cleared ECG recording and rhythm-classification algorithms, a HIPAA-compliant REST API (KardiaPro cloud) with webhook notifications for new ECG availability, and EHR integration (e.g. GE Healthcare MUSE NX). API/SDK access is gated behind a commercial agreement.
